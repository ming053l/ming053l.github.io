IrisMix: Compositional Makeup Transfer with Layered Diffusion Transformers

Chia-Ming Lee1,2 Ming-Ching Chang1 Xin Li1 Yu-Lun Liu2 Hong-Han Shuai2 Chih-Chung Hsu2

1 University at Albany, SUNY   2 National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University

Makeup is designed in rounds. IrisMix keeps it as an explicit layer, separate from the face, so a look can be composed from several references, rendered on a new face in one step, and still be edited afterwards.彩妝是一輪一輪調出來的。IrisMix 把妝容保留為與人臉分離的明確圖層：可從多張參考組合，一步渲染到新的人臉，完成後仍能繼續編輯。

01 / Highlights

Makeup as a layer, not an image妝容是圖層，不是一張圖

Regenerating an image for each revision can shift colors, alter identity and disturb regions that were already settled. Editing the layer leaves them alone.每次修改都重新生成，可能改變顏色和身份，也可能動到已經調好的區域。直接編輯圖層就不會影響它們。

01

Persistent editability持久可編輯

Rendering returns a layer rather than a fixed image. Part selection, stacking, recoloring and blending stay closed-form, and pixels outside the edited region are preserved.渲染後回傳的是圖層，不是固定的影像。選取部位、疊加、改色和混合都有封閉解，編輯區外的像素維持不變。

02

One-step layered transformer一步式圖層 Transformer

Two LoRAs on one frozen backbone: one decomposes a reference into a bare face and a layer, the other renders composed layers under the new scene illumination. Each runs in a single step.同一個凍結主幹上的兩個 LoRA：一個把參考分解成素顏與圖層，另一個在新場景光線下渲染組合後的圖層，各只需一步。

03

LayerBeauty datasetLayerBeauty 資料集

Made-up faces paired with their bare faces and exact layers, built from two complementary pipelines for creative and daily makeup.化妝人臉與其素顏、精確圖層成對，由創意妝與日常妝兩條互補流程建立。

03 / Editing

Keep editing after rendering渲染後 繼續編輯

Remove, replace, stack, recolor, move and blend, without disturbing the person or the scene.移除、更換、疊加、改色、移動、混合，都不會影響人物與場景。

Applications of the layer圖層的應用

Remove and replace makeup on the recovered bare face, stack sheer lip layers, and move, scale or mirror face paint before rendering.在回收的素顏上移除與更換妝容、疊加透明唇色，並在渲染前移動、縮放或鏡射彩繪。

Layer stacking圖層疊加

Layers composite bottom to top, and each can change strength or hue on its own.圖層由下往上合成，每層都能單獨調整強度或色相。

Look blending妝容混合

In-between frames interpolate the layers recovered from two renders, for whole looks and for lipstick shades.用兩次渲染回收的圖層做內插，整妝和唇色都適用。

Multi-round editing多輪編輯

Over seven edits on 30 looks, regenerating the previous output accumulates changes in skin, hair and background. Restarting from layers and the bare face keeps person and scene stable: consecutive-round drift outside the edited part is 0 to 0.38 ΔE for strength, hue, removal, restoration and layer order, against 1.95 to 9.06 for FLUX.2-klein. On a move, the two are close (1.66 and 1.55).對 30 組妝容做七種編輯時，重新生成上一輪輸出會累積膚色、頭髮與背景的變化；從圖層和素顏重新開始，人物與場景都較穩定。強度、色相、移除、還原與圖層順序這幾輪，編輯區外相鄰輪次的漂移為 0 至 0.38 ΔE，FLUX.2-klein 為 1.95 至 9.06；移動設計時兩者接近（1.66 與 1.55）。

Glasses and bangs眼鏡與瀏海

Occluders can be lifted off before transfer and restored afterwards, which keeps eye makeup behind the lenses and places hair over the design.遮擋物可在轉移前移開、完成後放回，保留鏡片後的眼妝，頭髮也會蓋在設計之上。

05 / Method

Decompose once, render in one step分解一次，一步渲染

Two LoRAs on one frozen MMDiT separate decomposition from rendering.同一個凍結 MMDiT 上的兩個 LoRA，分別負責分解與渲染。

STAGE I

Recover reusable layers回收可重用的圖層

A bare, white and black canvas predicted together determines coverage and color, so each reference is decomposed once. Training includes a harmonization cycle.同時預測素顏、白底與黑底，由此解出覆蓋率與顏色，每張參考只需分解一次。訓練時加入協調循環。

STAGE II

Compose and render組合並渲染

Selected layers are warped and composed on the source, then rendered in one Euler step, supervised by self-transfer through another face. Source pixels outside the editable region are kept and the layer is returned.選取的圖層對齊並合成到原圖上，再以一步 Euler 渲染，以經由另一張臉的自我轉移監督訓練。可編輯區外的原圖像素保持不變，並回傳圖層。

Layer anatomy圖層構成

Coverage and color separate the design from the skin, from daily washes to heavy face paint.覆蓋率與顏色把設計和皮膚分開，從日常淡妝到厚重彩繪都適用。

Effect of rendering渲染的效果

Compared with pasting the reference layer flat over the skin, Stage II keeps the design more coherent on the new face.相較於把參考圖層平貼在皮膚上，Stage II 讓設計在新臉上更連貫。

07 / Dataset

LayerBeauty

Editable makeup needs more than diverse portraits: it needs the bare face and the exact layer. All people in LayerBeauty are synthetic.可編輯的妝容不只需要多樣的人像，還需要素顏和精確圖層。LayerBeauty 中的人物都是合成的。

13,273CreativeScene looks, built makeup-firstCreativeScene 妝容，先生成妝容
4,794DailyScene looks, built bare-firstDailyScene 妝容，先生成素顏

Construction建立流程

Two pipelines, filtered for quality, produce pairs with explicit layers.兩條流程經品質篩選，產出附明確圖層的配對資料。

Pairs and layers配對與圖層

Each example has the made-up portrait, its paired bare face and its makeup layer.每筆資料包含化妝人像、對應素顏與彩妝圖層。

Limitations. Our layers omit view-dependent appearance, so large poses and closed eyes can disrupt transfer. Edits are limited to the design space spanned by the available reference layers. Future work will extend persistent, editable layers to temporally consistent video and 3D avatars.限制。圖層不包含隨視角改變的外觀，大角度轉頭和閉眼可能影響轉移。編輯範圍受限於現有參考圖層涵蓋的設計空間。未來會把持久可編輯圖層延伸到時間一致的影片與 3D 虛擬人。
