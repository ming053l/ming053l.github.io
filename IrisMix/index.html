BEHIND THE LOOK

Makeup you can
keep playing with.

IrisMix separates a reference look into an explicit makeup layer. Parts from different references can be combined on a new face, then refined and edited without starting over.

This playground uses prepared model results on a synthetic sample portrait. Selection, strength and color edits happen in your browser. New photos and new transfers require the full IrisMix pipeline.
